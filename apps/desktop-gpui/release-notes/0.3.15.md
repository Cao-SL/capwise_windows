## 封面、片尾和画面

- 「封面」集中管理片头和片尾，时间线也能直接进入编辑。背景和摄像头都在「画面」里。
- 人物默认展示办公风格。图片可右键锁定，双击后用边缘和角上的手柄裁剪。
- 在「自定义文字样式」弹窗里调整字体、字号、颜色、描边和文字背景。画布即时预览，点「应用」后保存。
- 在片尾拖动元素或撤销编辑时，播放头会留在片尾。

## 录制、字幕和缩放

- 录制悬浮窗的麦克风图标旁显示真实输入电平，说话时更容易看清变化。麦克风列表刷新和录制恢复放到后台处理。
- 「AI 字幕优化」一起处理明显识别错误和中文断句。尽量把书名留在同一条字幕里，去掉行尾逗号和句号，保留问号。
- 在暂停时点击字幕，预览保持全画面。播放和导出仍显示项目缩放。新生成的自动缩放更轻，短促的放大缩回更少。
- macOS 更新支持断点续传，对可用的旧版本优先下载增量包。

## Covers, outros, and framing

- Manage covers and outros from the Cover panel or the timeline. Background and camera now share the Frame panel.
- Office-style portraits appear first. Right-click an image to lock it or double-click to crop it with the edge and corner handles.
- Fine-tune font, size, fill, stroke, and text background in the Custom Text Style dialog. Preview changes on the canvas and select Apply to save them.
- Dragging elements and undoing edits on an outro keeps the playhead on that card.

## Recording, captions, and zoom

- The recording bar shows a clearer live microphone meter. Microphone list refresh and recording recovery run in the background.
- AI subtitle optimization corrects recognition errors and reflows Chinese captions. It keeps short book titles together and trims trailing commas and periods.
- Seeking from captions shows a steady full-frame preview while paused. Playback and export use the project zooms. Generated zooms are gentler and skip brief zoom pulses.
- macOS update downloads can resume and use a delta package when one is available.
